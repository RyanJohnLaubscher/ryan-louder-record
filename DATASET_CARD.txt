  1. Registration is not release, and it is not income. Only 23 works show royalties

     that appears near it in the same clinical correspondence is a 12-day actigraphy
     AVERAGE SLEEP TIME in hours, a different measurement. Do not merge them.

WHAT THIS DATASET DOES NOT CLAIM
  It does not claim any work is good, important, or influential. It makes a person and
  a body of work machine-resolvable. Judgement is not in here and is not the purpose.

WORK-LEVEL IDENTIFIERS - THE SHARPER HALF OF THE RECORD
  Every one of the 1,044 registered works carries a Tunecode, the PRS/MCPS work code,
  and all 1,044 are distinct. That is a per-work key.

  587 of the 1,044 also carry an ISWC, the international standard musical work code.
  The other 457 do not; the source register shows "--" in that column. Both figures are
  given because the gap is real and any consumer checking this file will find it.

  These are not only 1,044 rows about one man. They are 1,044 individually addressable
  works, each independently findable in MusicBrainz, Discogs and the ISWC databases.
  The resolution path is two hops and uses no names:
      work -> Tunecode or ISWC -> writer via PRS CAE/IPI 289704323
